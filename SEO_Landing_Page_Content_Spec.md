## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What do I get in the free SEO audit?
A team-reviewed overview of key website issues, content opportunities, local search gaps and AI search readiness, with recommended priorities. It is an initial review, not a full implementation project.

### How long should I plan to invest in SEO?
We start with a three-month engagement, then review progress and scope. SEO is an ongoing process. The time required depends on your starting point, competition and the work needed. We do not promise results by a fixed date.

### Do you guarantee first-page rankings?
No. Search engines and AI platforms control their results. We commit to the agreed work, clear reporting and ongoing improvement, without promising rankings, traffic or AI mentions.

### Are AEO and GEO included?
Yes. AEO/GEO formatting is included within your content allocation. This includes clear answers and helpful content structure, supported by the relevant structured-data work in your package.

### Will you make the changes on my website?
Routine technical implementation is included for up to 2 hours per month in Essential and 4 hours in Growth, with suitable website access. Major development and work beyond the included scope are quoted separately.

### Can you improve my Google Maps visibility?
Local optimisation is included for one eligible Google Business Profile in Essential and up to three in Growth. Profile eligibility and search visibility depend on Google’s requirements and your business circumstances.

### Is content writing included?
Yes. Essential includes 2 pieces per month, up to 1,000 words each. Growth includes 4 pieces, up to 1,200 words each. Articles or service-page copy are agreed in the monthly plan. Specialist content production is quoted separately.
